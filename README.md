# UK payroll deadlines 2026/27

Every UK payroll and tax deadline for the 2026/27 tax year, with the penalty
for missing each one.

**Live:** <https://dev-lab-x.github.io/uk-payroll-dates/>

Monthly: FPS on or before each payday, PAYE by the 19th (post) or 22nd
(electronic), CIS returns by the 19th.

Annual: 19 April final FPS · 31 May P60 · 6 July P11D · 19/22 July Class 1A ·
31 July second payment on account · 5 October SA registration · 31 October
paper return · 30 December coding-out cutoff · 31 January online return,
balancing payment and first payment on account.

## The two that catch people

**The 19th/22nd split.** Post is the 19th, electronic the 22nd — but if the
22nd is a weekend or bank holiday, cleared funds must arrive by the last
working day *before*. It does not roll forward.

**31 January is three deadlines at once.** The return for the year just gone,
the balancing payment for it, and the first payment on account for the year
you are in. People budget for the first and are caught by the third.

Tax-year boundaries are taken from
[uk-tax-rates](https://github.com/HadiDevLabx/uk-tax-rates).
